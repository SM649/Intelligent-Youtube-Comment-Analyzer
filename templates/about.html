Our System's Architecture

Our simple five-step process delivers powerful analytics in minutes

  1. Enter Video URL: Provide a valid YouTube video link to begin the analysis process. Our system supports any public YouTube video.
  2. Fetch Comments: Our system retrieves comments using the YouTube API, gathering thousands of data points for analysis.
  3. Analyze Text: NLP algorithms parse sentiment and keywords, categorizing comments and identifying trends in real-time.
  4. Display Results: Interactive charts and tables show insights including sentiment distribution, keyword frequency, and engagement metrics.
  5. Save History: Users can review past analyses in their profile, track changes over time, and export results for presentations.
